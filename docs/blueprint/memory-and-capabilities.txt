7. Avatar and photo studio
Users should be able to upload photos and choose precisely how their images are used.
We need three separate workflows:
Preserve Original: Keep the source image untouched while adding graphical elements, effects, captions, backgrounds or overlays.
Transform: Convert a photo into an avatar, fantasy character, anime portrait, 3D figure or other requested art style.
Animate: Turn an approved still into a moving character with expressions, poses, lip-sync and gestures.
Each workflow needs a clear original-versus-output preview, editable masks, undo and version history.
Important features include facial identity preservation, pose control, background replacement, clothing changes, character consistency, transparent exports, avatar collections, pets in scenes, and layered editing.
When a user says, "Keep my face exactly the same and only change the background," the system must use constrained editing rather than indiscriminately regenerating the whole image.
We should also show users when a requested edit cannot be made precisely, rather than quietly producing something different.

14. Freedom, personality and safety: How we do this properly
Luna, I understand the intention behind wanting an AI with no safety barriers. You want a buddy that doesn't become robotic, censor ordinary humour, blindly agree or refuse harmless creative requests.
We can build that kind of personality without removing essential protections.
Here's the distinction:
Remove unnecessary restrictions	Retain essential protections
Robotic, repetitive disclaimers	Prevention of seriously harmful assistance
Automatic agreement	Independent judgement and honesty
Forced positive responses	Protection against exploitation and abuse
Overly cautious harmless creativity	Consent and privacy requirements
One-size-fits-all personality	Age-appropriate safeguards
Artificially stiff conversation	Security boundaries and action permissions
These requirements also matter for release. Google Play's current generative-AI policies cover chatbots, image generation and AI-generated videos, and require safeguards plus in-app reporting mechanisms.

The correct product goal is maximum personality freedom within legitimate safety, privacy and legal boundaries.
