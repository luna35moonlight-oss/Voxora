## 6. One thing I'd add: Personality boundaries that change with context

Someone might enjoy strong sarcasm but dislike jokes about their family. Another person might appreciate encouragement but hate being called sweetheart.

So each buddy needs a private Communication Agreement that includes:

- Preferred name and forms of address.
- Humour, sarcasm and profanity preferences.
- Topics or styles to avoid.
- How directly the buddy should challenge the user's ideas.
- Voice and emotional tone preferences.
- Professional communication defaults.
- Optional faith-related preferences provided by the user.
- Corrections learned from feedback.

This agreement should be editable, not an invisible profile that Alpha invents.

And if someone tells their buddy, "Stop calling me bro," it should immediately respect that.
