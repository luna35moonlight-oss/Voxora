# VOXORA CAREER AGENT

New Module 22 | AI Job Hunter, Career Manager & Application Assistant

The vision: Your buddy doesn't just find vacancies. It researches opportunities, compares your experience against the requirements, prepares professional applications, helps submit them on supported platforms, tracks responses and prepares you for interviews.

Imagine saying:

> "Alpha, find me positions paying R30 000 or more, preferably remote or hybrid. You know my qualifications and experience. Find suitable opportunities, prepare my CV and applications, and show me what needs approval."

Alpha should then handle the research and preparation, not send you off to repeat everything manually.

## 2. Alpha's complete job application workflow

Find opportunities

Search selected job sites, company portals and job alerts. Filter by salary in ZAR, occupation, location, experience, remote work and qualifications.

Analyse suitability

Compare the user's verified work history and skills with the requirements. Explain strong matches, missing qualifications and potential concerns.

Prepare application

Tailor CVs, write cover letters, complete screening-question drafts and prepare supporting documents without inventing qualifications or experience.

Apply with authorisation

Submit through officially supported integrations or permitted application workflows after the user approves the application. Otherwise provide a prepared package and application link.

Track responses

Monitor authorised email correspondence, identify recruiter replies, update statuses and remind the user of follow-ups or interviews.

Prepare for interviews

Research the employer, simulate interviews through live voice and help practise answers, including salary negotiations.

## 4. Job application dashboard

Every application should remain traceable, with a separate history for unsuccessful and completed applications.

| Status             | What Alpha does                         |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Discovered         | Saves a suitable vacancy                |
| Preparing          | Tailors application documents           |

| Submitted          | Records verified submission and date    |
| Recruiter response | Alerts user and suggests next action    |
| Interview          | Creates preparation tasks and reminders |
| Offer received     | Helps evaluate the offer                |
| Unsuccessful       | Archives outcome, retains history       |

Alpha must detect duplicate vacancies across different sites and avoid submitting the same application repeatedly.

## 5. Important integration realities

I've checked the job platforms' published rules.

LinkedIn prohibits unauthorised bots and third-party software that automate activity on its website. Indeed also expressly prohibits third-party automated job applications outside approved integrations.&#x20;

PNet supports applications through jobseeker accounts, but that does not automatically give external applications permission to submit on a user's behalf.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://www.pnet.co.za\&sz=32)

General Terms and Conditions



So our architecture needs three submission routes:

- Direct submission: Use authorised APIs or supported integrations where available.
- Assisted application: Prepare everything and let the user complete restricted platform steps.
- Email application: Send completed applications to verified recruitment addresses after user approval.

The goal is to automate as much legitimate work as possible without getting people's recruitment accounts suspended.

## 6. Extra features I'm adding

I also want Voxora to include a Career Growth Engine.

It should analyse job-market requirements, identify skills the user could develop, suggest relevant courses, help build a professional portfolio and generate different CV versions for different career paths.

For example, your experience as a PA to a GM and an Onboarding & Compliance Officer could be presented differently for executive support, compliance, operations and administrative management roles, while keeping every claim factual.

And one more feature: Recruiter Relationship Manager.

Alpha should remember which recruiters the user has contacted, maintain follow-up histories, draft replies, detect interview invitations and prevent important opportunities from getting buried in an inbox.
