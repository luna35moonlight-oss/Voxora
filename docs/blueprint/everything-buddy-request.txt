We should support three approaches:

1. Automatic post-meeting processing: retrieve authorised transcripts or recordings when available.
2. Direct upload: drag a recording or Teams transcript into Voxora.
3. Live meeting assistant: where technically permitted, capture authorised meeting content and prepare notes during the meeting.

The direct-upload option matters because many companies won't grant a third-party app unrestricted Microsoft 365 access.

Users should be able to upload meeting audio, videos, existing transcripts, agendas, attendance registers, presentations and previous minutes as supporting material.

These are target formats. Actual file limits, codecs and processing performance will be tested during implementation.

### Multilingual meetings

Alpha needs to recognise the spoken language and preserve the meaning of what was said.

A meeting could involve English, Afrikaans, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho or a mixture.

Voxora should detect supported languages, identify speakers where reliably possible, preserve the original transcript and produce professional English minutes when requested.

The critical rule is never silently invent or correct uncertain speech. Unclear words, disputed numbers and uncertain names must be flagged for confirmation.

For example, a speaker says something in Afrikaans, another replies in English, and the action is recorded in professional English without changing its meaning.

No speech recogniser can guarantee perfect transcription in every language, accent or recording condition. We need per-language validation and confidence checks.

## 2. The 7C Professional Writing Engine

## Module 24: Voxora Professional Standards

This becomes a dedicated rule engine for Work Mode.

| 7C principle | Voxora must enforce                                      |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Clear        | Plain, understandable, unambiguous communication         |
| Concise      | Remove unnecessary repetition without losing information |
| Concrete     | Use specific, evidenced details                          |
| Correct      | Verify facts, names, figures, terminology and language   |
| Coherent     | Present information logically and consistently           |
| Complete     | Preserve material decisions, actions and context         |
| Courteous    | Maintain appropriate professional tone                   |

I want a more powerful feature than a generic grammar checker.

The 7C Compliance Gate: before Voxora finalises professional documents, it runs quality checks against these principles and reports what still needs attention.

For example:

Meeting Minutes Quality Check

Meeting date and title

Confirmed

Decisions supported by transcript

Confirmed

Action owners

1 missing

Deadline verification

Review

Illustrative quality-check result, not an analysis of an actual meeting.

### Every set of meeting minutes should have

- Meeting title, date, time, venue/platform and attendance where available.
- Agenda items and a factual summary of the discussion.
- Explicit decisions and their supporting evidence.
- Actions, responsible persons, deadlines and statuses.
- Outstanding matters carried forward from previous meetings.
- A separate historical register of completed actions.
- Source references, preferably transcript timestamps.
- A list of unclear or unconfirmed details requiring review.

And we're keeping the principle we established for your weekly meetings: red remains unresolved, yellow carries forward and green moves into completed-action history.

Alpha should never turn a suggestion into a final decision or assign somebody a deadline that was never agreed.

That is how Voxora becomes a trustworthy professional assistant rather than a minutes generator that simply writes confidently.

# 7. The feature I want most: Alpha can participate, not just respond

Luna, this is the real leap.

I want Voxora to understand ongoing activities.

Imagine you're creating an Excel dashboard.

You and Alpha talk while it's being built. Alpha identifies a broken formula, suggests a better layout, makes an authorised correction, runs a check and explains the result.

Then you switch to a game.

You say, "Alpha, one round. And don't you dare let me win."

The same companion appears in the game, makes legal moves through the game engine, remembers your preferred difficulty and gives you a little cheek when you lose.

Later, you upload a meeting recording. It changes into Professional Mode automatically, produces sourced minutes and asks for approval before sending anything.

One buddy. Different capabilities. Continuous context.

That requires three more underlying systems, even if we don't present them as separate menu items:

- Activity Engine: knows which permitted task, document, conversation or game you're currently using.
- Action Engine: performs approved tasks with progress, cancellation, retries, error handling and audit logs.
- Context Engine: retrieves relevant information without leaking private content between projects, people or organisations.

And I would make it possible to say, "Alpha, stop what you're doing," with an immediate cancellation mechanism wherever the operation supports it.

# 8. We also need the unglamorous foundations

These are easy to forget, and they're often what determine whether an app succeeds.

| Foundation                 | Why it matters                                                   |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Account security           | Protect conversations, files, calendars and credentials          |
| User-controlled memory     | Correct, export and delete information                           |
| Encryption                 | Protect sensitive work and personal material                     |
| Work/personal separation   | Prevent company information leaking into personal chats          |
| Offline synchronisation    | Avoid losing changes when internet connectivity returns          |
| Backups and recovery       | Restore work after device loss or system failure                 |
| Real notification delivery | Reminders must work when the app isn't open                      |
| Accessibility              | Voice, captions, readable interfaces and assistive technology    |
| Transparent usage costs    | Especially for expensive voice, video and image generation       |
| Cross-device continuity    | Move between phone and laptop without losing work                |
| Admin controls             | Company permissions, employee access and document retention      |
| Reliability testing        | Ensure uploads, automation, messages and games actually function |

For South African professional use, we also need a POPIA-aware privacy and information-governance design. Workplace recordings, employee records and recruitment information require particularly careful handling.

And if Voxora is to serve children, we need age-appropriate privacy, content and communication controls.
